Working approach

From ambiguity to release

1

Frame the problem

I collect constraints, product data, player feedback, and references—then separate symptoms from causes.

2

Build the plan

I translate a hypothesis into requirements, priorities, owners, acceptance criteria, and a release path.

3

Validate the result

I follow build quality, launch, and post-release signals, feeding the findings into the next iteration.
